## Hypothesis and expected result (before execution)

The full shift offers useful choices between personal tools, shared machinery,
the night bank and the final rush, and makes players want another run. Expect
players to explain why they spent or saved cash, describe a useful teammate
contribution, understand their results, and identify something to try next time.
These are hypotheses, not findings or numerical success thresholds. Choosing
not to buy an upgrade is valid evidence; inability to afford or discover it
must be recorded rather than bypassed with grants.

Correctness expectations: spending does not lower Crew Haul; upgrades and
funding cannot duplicate; hopper dirt keeps its identity/outcome; unsold finds
and unfinished dirt produce no payment at the bell; replay starts fresh.

## Proposed sessions for approval

One fresh full-length human run at each crew size: solo, two, three and four
players, followed by the replay/reset check. This is about 40 minutes of shift
time plus setup and debriefs; sessions may be split across days. This schedule
is a proposed experiment procedure, not a gameplay setting or passed result.

Use actual connected clients and record the number of independent human
operators. Prefer a different person per crew member for cooperative feedback.
If one person controls several clients, label that explicitly as owner-operated
multi-client evidence; do not represent it as independent-player cooperation.
If a crew size cannot be arranged, leave it pending and ask the owner to amend
the approved plan; synthetic members do not substitute for these human runs.

Record participant names/aliases, device/input, operator count, admitted crew
size, build, date, assistance and interruptions. Desktop is the proposed initial
input scope; do not claim touch/controller coverage unless actually tested.
Approval does not authorize automated gameplay, injected money, time skipping,
teleport-assisted progression or prebuilt machinery in the human sessions.

## During each natural shift

| Phase | What humans do | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| Opening/day | Dig, sift and sell; choose whether to buy tools or fund the sluice. In crew runs, try sharing dirt and roles. | First sale; each shovel/pan tier purchase; first contribution and completed sluice; who paid; reasons for spending or saving; confusion or assistance. |
| Machinery choices | If affordable, choose tier two, hopper or neither. Observe the stats/funding UI and bin feeding. | Time each add-on completes, purchase order, perceived benefit and opportunity cost. Record "not bought" and why when applicable. |
| Night, elapsed 5:00–8:00 | Decide whether to travel to the rich bank. Use or save its dirt; crews may divide transport/processing work. | Notice of opening, travel/use, reason to go or stay, whether the benefit feels worthwhile, and teamwork changes. |
| Dawn, elapsed 8:00 | Observe closure to new rich digs; process any retained rich dirt. | Clarity of closure and retained bonus; confusion or failure. If no rich dirt remains, mark that observation not exercised. |
| Rush, elapsed 9:00–10:00 | Decide between production, collection and returning to sell. | Whether countdown and faster work are noticed; changes in roles or decisions; clarity of the sell-before-bell warning. |
| Bell/results | Read the result screen before replay. Compare actual actions with contribution rows. | Crew Haul, unsold/unfinished items discarded without payment, each player's useful contributions, results clarity, and any incorrect/missing credit. |
| Replay | Each remaining player votes. Check the new run, then stop; another full shift is optional. | Cash/inventory/tool/machine/bin reset, day/night/rush reset, readiness/vote clarity and whether anyone is stranded. |

Do not force every purchase into every run. Record observed funding timings or
"not observed"; never estimate a missed timestamp. Missing feature coverage
must be listed at debrief and either covered by an approved follow-up run or
explicitly acknowledged in the owner's decision. No extra runs start silently.

## Debrief and decision

Ask each available human, without suggesting a preferred answer:

- Which spending choice mattered, and what did you give up by choosing it?
- Did the night bank or rush change your plan? Why or why not?
- In crew runs, what specifically did another player help with or get in the way of?
- Can you explain the result screen and what happened to unsold items?
- Would you play another shift now? What would you try differently?
- What was confusing, frustrating, broken or not worth buying?

Record answers as attributed reports. Separate observed facts, interpretation,
technical fixture results and missing evidence. Small owner playtests do not
establish statistical retention or public-launch readiness.

After reviewing the recorded runs, coverage gaps and any defects, yukicide
gives an explicit **continue** or **revise** decision with reasons. Transcribe
only the actual human decision into the next available D-### entry. Neither
passing gates nor plan approval implies approval to close Gate C.

Pause an affected run for crashes, broken progression, duplicated/lost money
or inventory, or a failed bell/replay; preserve logs and the point of failure.
Record incomplete runs honestly. Resolve blocking findings and rerun the
relevant checks before closeout. T-034 stays doing until its criteria have
evidence, then goes to a different human for independent review.
