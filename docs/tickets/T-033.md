## Why
Who uses it: crews splitting digging from processing. What breaks without it: Gate C's throughput-versus-handling choice between hopper and tier two is missing (DESIGN §8).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] The hopper moves dirt from the shared bin into the sluice queue per D-044
- [x] Funding follows D-044 (150 x N, needs the sluice, either order with tier two)
- [x] Feeding is atomic, never rerolls or duplicates dirt, and pauses when lanes or output are full
- [x] Studio solo/crew evidence and all gates pass

## Decisions
