## Goal
The player digs at the bank and carries dirt, using T-014 for batch rules, without exceeding capacity or rerolling a batch.
Source: DESIGN §5, 8, 12-13 (R12).

## Why
Who uses it: the player and sifting.
What breaks without it: clients or cancelled actions could create or duplicate dirt and loot.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A valid dig creates a batch only after its duration and within capacity; rejected distance/phase/rate/capacity requests change nothing — test: DIG-1
- [x] Batches keep server identity, source, digger, and assigned outcome through cancel and movement — test: DIG-2
- [x] Opening-protection counter is per player per run, survives avatar reset; starter gear is not regranted — test: DIG-3

## Out of scope
- Shared dirt bin, richer bank, machinery, terrain depletion
- Persistence

## Numbers used
- Values come from `src/shared/Config.luau` (D-011): approved `D-###`, else `PROVISIONAL DESIGN §N`. Gate A inventory: T-003 ND list.

## Test plan
DIG-1: `execute_luau` sends valid, forged, repeated, too-far, and full-bucket requests; compare state before/after. DIG-2: cancel and retry a known batch; expect identical fields. DIG-3: dig past the opening boundary, reset the avatar; expect counter kept, no extra gear.
