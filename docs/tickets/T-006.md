## Goal
The player digs and carries dirt without exceeding capacity or rerolling a batch.

Draft proposal only. Source: docs/DESIGN.md sections 5, 8, 12-13 (R12). Workstream/data contract: batch identity, source, owner, assigned outcome. This ticket authorizes no unanswered scope choice or provisional numeric value.

## Recorded owner decisions
D-002 establishes shared crew success; D-008 selects timing-based sifting for the subsequent processing step. No extra rolls arise from retries. Gate A batch/capacity quantities still need decisions.

## Why
Who uses it: the player and manual processing.
What breaks without it: clients or cancelled actions could create or duplicate dirt and loot.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A valid dig at the bank creates a batch only after its approved duration and within bucket capacity; rejected distance/phase/rate/capacity requests change no inventory — test: DIG-1
- [ ] Each created batch retains server identity, source, original digger, and assigned find/crate outcome; cancellation/movement does not reroll it — test: DIG-2
- [ ] The approved opening-protection counter is per player/run and is not reset by avatar reset; starter gear is not granted repeatedly — test: DIG-3

## Out of scope
- Shared dirt bin, richer bank, machinery, terrain depletion mechanics not approved in T-004
- Permanent discoveries or profile persistence
- Features outside the owner-approved Gate A boundary; P1/P2 candidates.
