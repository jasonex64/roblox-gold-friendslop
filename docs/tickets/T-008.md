## Goal
The player sells completed finds and buys the Gate A upgrade at camp, with all money math done by T-016.
Source: DESIGN §5-8, 13-15 (Gate A, R04/R12); D-002, D-003, D-007.

## Why
Who uses it: the player and the result screen.
What breaks without it: sales or purchases could duplicate value or reduce the score.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Sell-all consumes finds once, then adds base values to Crew Haul and the solo wallet; repeated or forged requests grant nothing extra — test: ECON-1
- [x] The upgrade checks affordability, deducts its price once, and changes the rate for later batches — test: ECON-2
- [x] Purchases never reduce Crew Haul; rejected purchases change nothing; UI labels the upgrade temporary — test: ECON-3

## Out of scope
- Crew splits and contributions (Gate B)
- Paid multipliers, Robux, Claim Tokens, new currencies

## Numbers used
- Values come from `src/shared/Config.luau` (D-011): approved `D-###`, else `PROVISIONAL DESIGN §N`. Gate A inventory: T-003 ND list.

## Test plan
ECON-1: sell a known fixture twice and send a forged value; expect one grant. ECON-2: buy with too little then enough cash, then dig; expect one deduction and the new rate. ECON-3: compare Crew Haul around purchases.
