Source: DESIGN §5-8, 13-15 (Gate A, R04/R12); D-002, D-003, D-007.

## Why

What breaks without it: sales or purchases could duplicate value or reduce the score.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Sell-all consumes finds once, then adds base values to Crew Haul and the solo wallet; repeated or forged requests grant nothing extra — test: ECON-1
- [ ] The upgrade checks affordability, deducts its price once, and changes the rate for later batches — test: ECON-2
- [ ] Purchases never reduce Crew Haul; rejected purchases change nothing; UI labels the upgrade temporary — test: ECON-3
