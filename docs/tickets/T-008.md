## Goal
The player sells completed finds, sees haul and wallet balances, and buys the approved temporary upgrade.

Draft proposal only. Source: docs/DESIGN.md sections 5-8, 13-15 (Gate A, R04/R12). Workstream/data contract: atomic sale, wallet, cumulative haul, tool tier. This ticket authorizes no unanswered scope choice or provisional numeric value.

## Why
Who uses it: the player and result system.
What breaks without it: sales or purchases could duplicate value or subtract spending from the score.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Sell all at camp consumes completed finds once before adding their approved base values to Crew Haul and the eligible solo wallet; duplicate requests and client-supplied values cannot grant money — test: ECON-1
