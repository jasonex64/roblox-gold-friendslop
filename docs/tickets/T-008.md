## Goal
The player sells completed finds, sees haul and wallet balances, and buys the approved temporary upgrade.

Draft proposal only. Source: docs/DESIGN.md sections 5-8, 13-15 (Gate A, R04/R12). Workstream/data contract: atomic sale, wallet, cumulative haul, tool tier. This ticket authorizes no unanswered scope choice or provisional numeric value.

## Why
Who uses it: the player and result system.
What breaks without it: sales or purchases could duplicate value or subtract spending from the score.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Sell all at camp consumes completed finds once before adding their approved base values to Crew Haul and the eligible solo wallet; duplicate requests and client-supplied values cannot grant money — test: ECON-1
- [ ] The owner-selected existing shovel/pan upgrade checks affordability and prerequisites, deducts only the approved price, and changes the documented action rate for subsequent batches — test: ECON-2
- [ ] Purchases never subtract from cumulative Crew Haul; rejected purchases leave all state unchanged and the UI clearly labels the upgrade as temporary — test: ECON-3

## Out of scope
- Crew cash splitting/funding, machines, paid multipliers, Robux purchases
- Claim Tokens, class shops, new currencies, invented upgrades or prices
- Features outside the owner-approved Gate A boundary; P1/P2 candidates.

## Numbers used
- No approved gameplay values yet. Needs decision: applicable quantities in T-003 Log (ND01-ND38). Before implementing or running numeric acceptance checks, cite human-approved D-### entries; do not copy provisional DESIGN.md values as defaults.

## Test plan
ECON-1: sell a known approved output fixture, repeat the request, and forge a sale value; expect one grant and correct score. ECON-2: attempt the approved upgrade with insufficient and sufficient funds, then start another batch; expect one deduction and the D-cited effect. ECON-3: compare Crew Haul before/after purchase and rejected requests; expect unchanged haul and clear reset labeling. Values and numeric/rounding bounds come only from T-004 D decisions.
