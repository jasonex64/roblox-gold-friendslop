## Goal
A player can discover and perform dig, sift, sell, upgrade, and finish using the documented controls.

Draft proposal only. Source: docs/DESIGN.md sections 1, 5, 14-16 (Gate A, applicable R01/R13). Workstream/data contract: action requests and server-state presentation. This ticket authorizes no unanswered scope choice or provisional numeric value.

## Why
Who uses it: first-time players.
What breaks without it: a correct server loop would still be inaccessible or misleading.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Contextual actions support the owner-approved keyboard/mouse and touch/input-mapping scope without rapid repeated clicking; invalid actions show a clear reason — test: UX-1
- [ ] During the run, show timer, applicable phase, Crew Haul, personal cash, bucket fill, and the next relevant action from authoritative state — test: UX-2
- [ ] Contextual guidance explains dig/sift/sell/upgrade, temporary purchases and end-of-run disposal; core actions do not depend on hover or sound alone — test: UX-3

## Out of scope
- Full class/pet/lobby/monetization screens, later-gate polish or performance claims
- A skill minigame or telemetry platform absent from the approved scope
- Features outside the owner-approved Gate A boundary; P1/P2 candidates.
