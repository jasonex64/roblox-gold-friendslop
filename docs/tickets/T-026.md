## Goal
`src/shared/Pure/Contributions.luau` records each player's digging, processing, feeding, selling and funding separately during a shift and returns a per-player summary for the Gate C results screen.
Source: DESIGN §6 (contribution credit), §4 (result screen), §15 Gate C. Pure so it runs in parallel with T-021.

## Why
Who uses it: the later Gate C results-screen wiring ticket.
What breaks without it: credit counting ends up spread across server handlers, where retries double-count and departed players' work disappears.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `record` adds a positive amount to one player's dug, processed, loaded, sold or funded total; repeated event IDs change nothing; unknown kinds and non-positive amounts error — test: CREDIT-1, CREDIT-2
- [ ] `summary` lists every player who contributed, in first-contribution order, including players who left; sold totals add up to Crew Haul — test: CREDIT-3
