#### Unanswered design questions
- **Q01** Design section 17.1: shared Crew Haul with equal sale shares, or competition for individual wealth? The draft default is not an owner decision.
- **Q02** Section 17.2: personal wallets with voluntary machinery funding, or a shared wallet?
- **Q03** Section 17.3: relaxed mining, production rush, or survival; should night only offer a richer bank? No hazards are added to this plan.
- **Q04** Section 17.4: how strong may paid power be, and are personal records sufficient or is serious standardized competition wanted later?
- **Q05** Section 17.5: do class rerolls replace a class or add permanent ownership; are there rarity tiers?
- **Q06** Section 17.6: does items worth 2x mean personal sale cash, team income, or a premium item?
- **Q07** Section 17.7: hold/toggle sifting or a timing-based skill interaction? The tickets describe the documented hold/toggle proposal conditionally.
- **Q08** Section 17.8: who is implementing the game, with which experience, art resources, target devices, and availability? No dates or workstream owners are inferred.
- **Q09** Section 17.9: is limited same-live-server reconnect sufficient, or is crash-resume required? Crash-resume is excluded from this draft M1.
- **Q10** Sections 3 and 15: approve M1 as Gate A only (the smallest playable manual run), or must the first milestone already prove cooperation (Gate B) or the full core validation MVP (A-C)? Gate A is a proposed planning boundary, not a final scope decision.
- **Q11** Sections 5, 9, and 15: Gate A precedes classes, but new players receive Prospector and its dig bonus. Does the Gate A starter loadout apply that bonus, or use neutral base rates? Do the tool durations in section 8 precede all modifiers?
- **Q12** Sections 4 and 15: does Gate A use an unchanging daylight scene with only the run-end cutoff, leaving night/final-surge phases to Gate C? Which result-screen fields should be omitted until their later-gate systems exist?
- **Q13** Sections 4, 12, and 13: must Gate A persist any result before showing it, or is its run-local result/in-place reset sufficient until durable rewards in Gate D? A session result must never imply unsaved permanent rewards.
- **Q14** Sections 5 and 8: are all listed finds and the nested Mystery Crate required in Gate A, or is an owner-approved subset sufficient? The plan does not select an arbitrary subset.
- **Q15** Sections 5, 8, and 15: which documented shovel/pan upgrade is the Gate A purchase, and is the free shared dirt bin deferred with Gate B? Both starter tools remain available; no new upgrade is invented.
- **Q16** Sections 5 and 12: on cancel, reset, full output, or moving away, precisely where does the reserved batch return and when is its output slot released? Preserve its assigned outcome; do not silently discard it except at the bell as specified.
- **Q17** Section 6: what fixed-point precision and deterministic remainder recipient ordering should sales use? What rounding applies to source bonuses and modifier calculations?
- **Q18** Sections 4 and 5: at the exact end timestamp, how are simultaneous completion, sell, purchase, and cancellation requests ordered? Completion at or before the end counts; define the remaining tie rules without giving a client authority.
- **Q19** Sections 6 and 12: does collection eligibility exactly equal cash-share eligibility at reveal, and how are grace, disconnect expiry, and bell eligibility reconciled?
- **Q20** Sections 9 and 10: when an eligible storage-bonus owner leaves or becomes inactive, what happens to dirt already exceeding the reduced shared-bin capacity?
- **Q21** Sections 12 and 13: what are the bounded settlement retry policy, timeout/failure limits, and profile-lock/handoff rules? WORKFLOW requires human decisions before retry limits are implemented.
- **Q22** Sections 13, 14, and 16: choose the baseline phone, performance test setup, usability cohort, and measurement thresholds; is controller compatibility an input-mapping requirement only or a separately supported launch device?
- **Q23** Sections 5, 8, and 13: specify missing interaction distances, request-rate limits, numeric bounds, deposit supply/refresh, movement assumptions, and starting Run Cash. They cannot be derived safely from the example tuning table.
- **Q24** Sections 3, 15, and 16: confirm which launch metrics, touch polish, purchase tests, and save/recovery checks are later-gate requirements rather than Gate A exit criteria. The draft does not treat the broader public-MVP goals as already achieved.
